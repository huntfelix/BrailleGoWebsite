Free to build, change and sell

BrailleGo! is open source hardware. Anyone can make one, improve it or sell it. In return, we ask two things: credit the original, and share your changes under the same terms so everyone benefits.

What you can and must do

You can

  • Build a BrailleGo! for yourself, a school, a library or a charity
  • Change the design, fix it or add features
  • Make and sell printers, kits or circuit boards
  • Translate the guides and share them

If you share or sell it, you must

  • Keep our copyright and licence notices on the files and on the board
  • Publish the source files for your version (design files and firmware code) under the same licences
  • Mark which files you changed and when
  • Give buyers a link to where they can get those files

Building one just for yourself? You don’t have to publish anything, though we’d love to see it.

Contributing

Improvements are welcome. Contributions are accepted under the same licence as the part of the project they change. Please sign off each commit (git commit -s) to confirm you have the right to submit it, following the Developer Certificate of Origin.

Other people’s work

Anything we use from other projects, such as libraries in the firmware, keeps its own licence. We note these next to the files in the repository.

No warranty, and please build safely

BrailleGo! is a student project shared “as is”, without any warranty. All three licences say so in full, and you build and use it at your own risk.
