Submit a build report

Built a BrailleGo!, forked the design or deployed one in a school, library or charity? Send us a build report. We’ll review it and publish the best ones here, with credit to you.

Submit a build report

Opens an email to Tom and Felix with a report template filled in. Attach your photos before sending.

What to include

  • Hardware revision, or a link to your fork
  • Changes to the CAD, PCB or bill of materials
  • Firmware version or commit hash
  • Calibration settings and print parameters
  • Embossing results: paper weight, dot height, speed
  • Faults you hit and how you fixed them
  • Photos of the build and an embossed page
